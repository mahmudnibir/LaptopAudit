How it works

01 / COLLECT

Run quick-check

Generate a local JSON snapshot of the device, battery, memory, CPU, GPU, and storage.

02 / INSPECT

Look closer

Open the physical checklist and record what you observe on the actual laptop.

03 / DECIDE

Share carefully

Generate an HTML report, review its private hardware details, and share only what you choose.

What gets checked

Battery and memoryCapacity, health where available, charge state, modules, speed, and available RAM.
StorageDrive model, estimated capacity, type, interface, and available SMART prediction.
CPU and graphicsProcessor model, cores, threads, graphics adapter, VRAM, and driver version.
Physical conditionDisplay, keyboard, trackpad, camera, audio, ports, wireless, hinges, and body.
Evidence over false precision. Missing hardware data is shown as unavailable. LaptopAudit does not invent a universal score or claim that software can verify physical condition.
